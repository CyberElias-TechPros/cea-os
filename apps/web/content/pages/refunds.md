Every digital product in the CEA shop comes with a 14-day refund window. No return parcel, no restocking fee, no penalty for changing your mind.The policy

## What we refund, when and how

Plain English. If anything below is unclear, message us and we will explain on the call.

### 14 days, change of mind included

If you change your mind for any reason within 14 days of purchase, ask for a refund and we will process it. No questions, no return parcel.

### Refund to the original method

Money goes back the way it came in: the same Paystack card, transfer or USSD channel. Refunds land within 30 days of approval.

### One email is enough

Reply to your order email or write to [[email protected]](https://www.cea.ng/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) with your order reference. We confirm within one working day.

## Eligibility

You can request a refund within 14 days of the purchase date for any digital product in the shop. You do not need to give a reason. If the file is defective or does not match the description on the product page, the 14-day window still applies — but we will fix or replace the file first if that is what you would prefer.

## How to ask

Reply to the order confirmation email or write to [[email protected]](https://www.cea.ng/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3a525f5656557a595f5b14545d) with your order reference (it starts with cea_shop_). We confirm receipt within one working day and process approved refunds within 30 days.

## What is refunded

The full purchase price in naira. Paystack charges are not refunded to us, so you receive the full amount you paid. There is no restocking fee because nothing physical is shipped.

## Delivery method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method — the same card, bank account or USSD channel used at checkout. We do not refund to a different account for fraud-prevention reasons.

## After the 14-day window

If more than 14 days have passed, we still want to hear from you. We will review defective files and obvious errors case-by-case. The 14-day rule is a floor, not a ceiling — we treat each request on its merits.
